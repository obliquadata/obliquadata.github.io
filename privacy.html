Privacy

World Leaderle is a static browser game hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not require an account and it does not ask players to enter passwords, payment information, phone numbers, or other sensitive personal data.

The game stores a small amount of local browser data such as theme choice, settings, daily progress, and random-mode progress so the game can remember your preferences on your own device.

Leader summaries and related public information are fetched from public sources such as Wikipedia. Those requests are used to display gameplay content, not to collect personal information from players.
