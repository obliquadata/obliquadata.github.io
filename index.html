About

Built to investigate difficult, interdisciplinary problems.

Obliqua Data is a small studio focused on challenges shaped by space, systems, uncertainty, and public decision-making. The emphasis is on using evidence well: to map, model, compare, and communicate complex issues clearly.

Focus areas
  • Spatial analysis and mapping
  • Climate and sustainability questions
  • Infrastructure and mobility systems
  • Policy trade-off analysis
Approach

The studio combines quantitative analysis with careful framing and communication, to turn complex evidence into something decision-makers can actually use.
