How to play

Corruption hintAfter a wrong guess, you are told whether your guessed country is more corrupt, similarly corrupt, or less corrupt than the mystery country.
Continent hintYou are also told whether the guessed country is on the correct continent.
Distance hintEach guess gets a 5-square emoji bar that becomes greener as the guess gets geographically closer.
Serving hintThe top right of the portrait indicates how long the leader has served in their current position.

Implementation notes

Summary strategy

This version keeps data/leaders.json lightweight and only fetches a Wikipedia summary when a leader is revealed. That avoids mass rate limits during scheduled database generation.

Expected JSON shape

{ "generatedAt": "...", "count": 198, "leaders": [ { "id": "...", "leader": "...", "country": "...", "role": "Head of state", "articleTitle": "...", "image": "...", "continent": "...", "startDate": "YYYY-MM-DD", "coords": { "lat": 0, "lon": 0 }, "corruptionScore": 0.0, "summary": "" } ] }
